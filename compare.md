Before choosing an iPhone app for similar photos, check how it selects a keeper, confirms deletion, protects your photos and charges for use. These questions describe how Alike handles each decision.

## What to check before you choose

| Question | Why it matters | Alike |
|---|---|---|
| **Does it find similar photos, or only exact copies?** | Bursts, repeated selfies and several attempts at the same view can be close without being identical. | Finds near-duplicates and groups them by time, place and look. You set how strict the match is. |
| **Does it pick the best shot for you?** | Comparing eight near-identical photos by eye is the slow part. | Picks one photo per group by sharpness, exposure, faces and noise, and learns from the shots you choose — on the device. |
| **What happens when you delete?** | A wrong tap should not cost you a photo. | Nothing goes without your confirmation, and iOS asks once more. Removed photos go to Recently Deleted, where iOS keeps them for about 30 days. |
| **Where are your photos analysed?** | If photos or image data go to a server, they leave your device. Check the listing and the privacy policy. | On your iPhone only, with Apple's Vision framework. Alike works in Airplane Mode. |
| **What does the App Privacy label say?** | The label lists data the developer reports collecting, including data used to track you. | Data Not Collected. No analytics, no tracking. |
| **Do you need an account? Are there ads?** | Check whether you need to register and whether the app shows ads. | No account, no sign-in, no ads. |
| **How does it charge?** | Check the billing period and trial eligibility before subscribing. | Free with 3 scans a month. Alike Pro has monthly and yearly plans in your local currency. The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. No weekly plan. |
| **Can you see what you will free before you start?** | It shows whether a cleanup is worth your time. | Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show the space you can reclaim. |

*The App Store shows the current price in your local currency.*

## Is Alike the right one for you

**Alike fits** if your camera roll is full of bursts and repeated shots, you want the app to choose the keeper, and you want photo analysis to stay on your iPhone.

**Look elsewhere** if you mainly need to compress videos or convert Live Photos — Alike does not do that — or if you want a one-time purchase instead of a subscription.

## How Alike decides

- **Grouping.** Alike compares photos taken close together in time and place, so a burst, a series of selfies or ten tries at the same sunset land in one group.
- **Best Shot.** In each group, Alike scores sharpness, exposure, faces and noise and marks the strongest photo. When you pick a different one, Alike learns from it on the device. You can reset what it learned in Settings.
- **Review.** Keep Best Only, Select All Except Best, or choose by hand.
- **Free and Pro.** Free includes Best Shot, enhancement and the widgets, and cleans one photo at a time. Pro adds unlimited scans, batch cleanup, screenshot and blurred-photo cleanup, and advanced filters.
- **Privacy.** No upload, no account, no analytics, no ads. Read the [Privacy Policy]({{ t.privacy_url | relative_url }}) for the details.
